Project

The Cinema Archive is a multi-page movie encyclopedia inspired by IMDb, Letterboxd. Structure comes from semantic elements: headings, tables, lists, forms and expandable details sections.

It currently contains 15 films, 6 directors and 24 actors.

Data sources and verification status

Intended reference sources are Wikidata, Wikipedia, Wikimedia Commons, official studio and film websites, the Academy Awards Database and IMDb (for cross-checking only; no IMDb content or images were copied).

Important limitation: The facts here (dates, runtimes, credits, awards, production facts) were therefore written from the editor's general knowledge of the public record and were not cross-checked live. Runtimes, release dates for specific territories, and production-company lists are the details most worth re-verifying. Synopses are original wording written for this archive.

Each movie page lists where to verify its entry, and SOURCES.md collects the same information.

Image credits

No images are included. This is intentional:

  • The build environment could not download from Wikimedia Commons, so no licence could be verified.
  • Official film posters are copyrighted and are generally not freely licensed on Wikimedia Commons, so they would not have been eligible even with access.
  • IMDb images were not used, in line with IMDb's terms.

The folders images/posters, images/directors, images/actors and images/gallery are reserved for future, licence-verified files. Any added image must be recorded in SOURCES.md with its URL, creator and licence.

Audio and video

No audio or video is embedded: official trailers and clips are not available under licences that allow redistribution inside a downloadable offline project.

Technical constraints

  • Pure HTML5; no style attributes or elements, no stylesheets, no scripts, no event-handler attributes.
  • All navigation uses ordinary hyperlinks with relative paths, so the site works from a local folder with no internet connection.
  • Appearance is controlled entirely by the browser.
