About Cinematic Encyclopedia

The project

Cinematic Encyclopedia is a small movie encyclopedia inspired by IMDb, Letterboxd and The Movie Database. It is a student project built by hand in plain HTML.

It currently contains 9 films and 5 directors. Each film has its own page with key facts, a spoiler-free synopsis, main cast, awards, production facts and related films.

Data sources

Reference sources for the film information are Wikipedia, Wikidata, the Academy Awards database, and official studio and film websites.

Please note: the facts on these pages (dates, runtimes, credits, awards and production facts) were written from general knowledge and have not all been checked against these sources. Release dates, runtimes, countries of production and award counts are the details most worth re-checking. Synopses are written in original wording. Each film page links to its Wikipedia article so you can verify the details yourself.

Image credits

Movie posters and the banner image are shown for illustration in this non-commercial student project. Each poster belongs to its respective studio and rights holders. No claim of ownership is made.

If you are a rights holder and would like an image removed, please use the contact page.

Trailers

Trailers are not embedded. The "Watch the trailer" link on each film page opens a YouTube search for the official trailer.
