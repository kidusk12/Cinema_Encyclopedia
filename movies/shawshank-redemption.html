Plot synopsis

Over many years in Shawshank State Penitentiary, a banker convicted of murdering his wife forms an enduring friendship with a fellow inmate and finds ways to hold on to hope.

Awards and nominations

  • Received 7 Academy Award nominations, including Best Picture, Best Actor (Morgan Freeman) and Best Adapted Screenplay. It did not win any.

Production facts

  • Adapted from a novella in Stephen King's 1982 collection Different Seasons.
  • Filmed largely at the Ohio State Reformatory in Mansfield, Ohio.
  • Its box-office run was modest; it found a wide audience through home video and television.
  • Selected for the U.S. National Film Registry in 2015.
