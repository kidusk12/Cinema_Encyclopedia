Plot synopsis

Paul Atreides, the son of a noble family, travels with his house to the desert planet Arrakis, the only source of the most valuable substance in the universe, where political rivalries and an ancient native culture collide.

Awards and nominations

  • Won 6 Academy Awards: Best Cinematography, Best Film Editing, Best Production Design, Best Sound, Best Original Score and Best Visual Effects.
  • Received 10 Academy Award nominations in total, including Best Picture and Best Adapted Screenplay.

Production facts

  • Covers roughly the first half of Frank Herbert's 1965 novel; the story continues in Dune: Part Two (2024).
  • Desert scenes were filmed in Jordan and Abu Dhabi.
  • The score is by Hans Zimmer.
  • It premiered at the Venice Film Festival in September 2021.
