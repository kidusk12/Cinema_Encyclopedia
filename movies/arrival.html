Plot synopsis

When twelve mysterious spacecraft appear across the globe, a linguist is recruited by the military to find a way to communicate with the visitors and learn why they have come.

Awards and nominations

  • Won the Academy Award for Best Sound Editing (Sylvain Bellemare).
  • Received 8 Academy Award nominations in total, including Best Picture, Best Director and Best Adapted Screenplay.

Production facts

  • Adapted from Ted Chiang's 1998 novella "Story of Your Life".
  • Filmed in Quebec, Canada.
  • The score is by Jóhann Jóhannsson; the film also uses Max Richter's "On the Nature of Daylight".
  • It premiered at the Venice Film Festival in September 2016.
