Directors

Christopher Nolan

British-American
Born 30 July 1970, London, England

Christopher Nolan first drew attention with the low-budget thriller Following (1998) and the non-linear mystery Memento (2000). He then directed the Dark Knight trilogy, followed by the original science-fiction films Inception and Interstellar, and later Dunkirk, Tenet and Oppenheimer.

Films

  • Following (1998)
  • Memento (2000)
  • Insomnia (2002)
  • Batman Begins (2005)
  • The Prestige (2006)
  • The Dark Knight (2008)
  • Inception (2010)
  • The Dark Knight Rises (2012)
  • Interstellar (2014)
  • Dunkirk (2017)
  • Tenet (2020)
  • Oppenheimer (2023)

David Fincher

American
Born 28 August 1962, Denver, Colorado, USA

David Fincher began his career in visual effects at Industrial Light & Magic before directing music videos and commercials. His first feature was Alien 3 (1992); he established his reputation with Se7en and Fight Club.

Films

  • Alien 3 (1992)
  • Se7en (1995)
  • The Game (1997)
  • Fight Club (1999)
  • Panic Room (2002)
  • Zodiac (2007)
  • The Curious Case of Benjamin Button (2008)
  • The Social Network (2010)
  • The Girl with the Dragon Tattoo (2011)
  • Gone Girl (2014)
  • Mank (2020)
  • The Killer (2023)

Denis Villeneuve

Canadian
Born 3 October 1967, Bécancour, Quebec, Canada

Denis Villeneuve emerged from the Quebec film scene with Un 32 août sur terre, Maelström, Polytechnique and Incendies, the last of which was nominated for the Academy Award for Best Foreign Language Film. He went on to make English-language thrillers and large-scale science fiction.

Films

  • Un 32 août sur terre (1998)
  • Maelström (2000)
  • Polytechnique (2009)
  • Incendies (2010)
  • Prisoners (2013)
  • Enemy (2013)
  • Sicario (2015)
  • Arrival (2016)
  • Blade Runner 2049 (2017)
  • Dune (2021)
  • Dune: Part Two (2024)

Quentin Tarantino

American
Born 27 March 1963, Knoxville, Tennessee, USA

Quentin Tarantino worked in a Los Angeles video store before writing and directing Reservoir Dogs (1992). Pulp Fiction won the Palme d'Or at Cannes and the Academy Award for Best Original Screenplay, which he shared with Roger Avary.

Films

  • Reservoir Dogs (1992)
  • Pulp Fiction (1994)
  • Jackie Brown (1997)
  • Kill Bill: Vol. 1 (2003)
  • Kill Bill: Vol. 2 (2004)
  • Death Proof (2007)
  • Inglourious Basterds (2009)
  • Django Unchained (2012)
  • The Hateful Eight (2015)
  • Once Upon a Time in Hollywood (2019)

Frank Darabont

American (born in France to Hungarian parents)
Born 28 January 1959, Montbéliard, France

Frank Darabont grew up in Los Angeles and first worked as a screenwriter on horror films before adapting Stephen King's novella for his directorial debut, The Shawshank Redemption. He is also known for developing the television series The Walking Dead.

Films

  • The Shawshank Redemption (1994)
  • The Green Mile (1999)
  • The Majestic (2001)
  • The Mist (2007)
