Biography

Frank Darabont was born in a refugee camp in Montbéliard, France, to Hungarian parents who had left Hungary after the 1956 revolution, and grew up in Los Angeles. He first worked as a screenwriter on horror films before adapting Stephen King's novella for his directorial debut, The Shawshank Redemption (1994).

He went on to direct The Green Mile, The Majestic and The Mist, and developed the television series The Walking Dead. He received Academy Award nominations for the adapted screenplays of The Shawshank Redemption and The Green Mile.
