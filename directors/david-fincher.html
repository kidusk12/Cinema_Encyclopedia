Biography

David Fincher began his career in visual effects at Industrial Light & Magic before directing music videos and commercials. His first feature was Alien 3 (1992); he established his reputation with Se7en (1995) and Fight Club (1999).

His later work includes Zodiac, The Curious Case of Benjamin Button, The Social Network and Gone Girl. He has been nominated for the Academy Award for Best Director several times.
