Biography

Denis Villeneuve emerged from the Quebec film scene with Un 32 août sur terre (1998), Maelström (2000), Polytechnique (2009) and Incendies (2010), the last of which was nominated for the Academy Award for Best Foreign Language Film.

He moved to English-language cinema with Prisoners (2013) and went on to direct Enemy, Sicario, Arrival, Blade Runner 2049 and the two-part adaptation of Frank Herbert's Dune. He was nominated for the Best Director Oscar for Arrival.
