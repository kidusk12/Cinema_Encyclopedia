Biography

Christopher Edward Nolan first drew attention with the low-budget thriller Following (1998) and the non-linear mystery Memento (2000). He then directed the Dark Knight trilogy for Warner Bros., followed by the original science-fiction films Inception and Interstellar, and later Dunkirk, Tenet and Oppenheimer.

Nolan co-founded the production company Syncopy with Emma Thomas. He is known for championing photochemical film and large-format IMAX photography. He won the Academy Awards for Best Director and Best Picture for Oppenheimer (2023).
