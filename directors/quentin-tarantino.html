Biography

Quentin Tarantino worked in a Los Angeles video store before writing and directing Reservoir Dogs (1992). Pulp Fiction (1994) won the Palme d'Or at Cannes and the Academy Award for Best Original Screenplay, which he shared with Roger Avary.

His later films include Jackie Brown, Kill Bill, Death Proof, Inglourious Basterds, Django Unchained, The Hateful Eight and Once Upon a Time in Hollywood. He has won two Academy Awards for Best Original Screenplay (Pulp Fiction and Django Unchained).
